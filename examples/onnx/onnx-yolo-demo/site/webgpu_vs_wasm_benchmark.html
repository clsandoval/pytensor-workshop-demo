See the Difference: Trained Ultralytics vs Untrained PyTensor ONNX Export
This demo shows two YOLO11n models side-by-side: the official Ultralytics model fully trained on the COCO dataset (yolo11n_320.onnx) that can accurately detect 80 object classes, and an untrained PyTensor ONNX export (yolo11n.onnx) with random weights that produces meaningless detections.
